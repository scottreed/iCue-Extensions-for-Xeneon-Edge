# Ember shards - my notes (my own play notes)
Game: Ember Harbor (PC). There are 8 ember shards to collect. I measured the pixel positions of some of them on my map image (map.png, 1000 x 700 pixels, origin at the top left) with an image editor.

1. Pier shard - x 140, y 520. Next to the broken boat on the pier. MISSABLE: it disappears once you talk to the ferryman.
2. Net rack shard - x 255, y 430. Behind the net rack, easy to miss.
3. Warehouse shard - x 410, y 360. Inside the warehouse, needs the rusty key.
4. Furnace shard - x 640, y 280. In the furnace room, you have to light the furnace first.
5. Tower shard - x 780, y 120. Dropped by the Keeper. Point of no return: you can't get it after you break the lamp.
6. Cave shard - somewhere in the north cave, behind the waterfall. I never measured it.
7. Lighthouse cellar shard - under the lighthouse, I didn't measure this one either.
8. Gull nest shard - on the east cliffs, near the gulls. No position measured.
