# Ember Harbor - my play notes (written by me, for myself)
Game: Ember Harbor, PC, normal difficulty. I'm happy with spoilers.

## Chapter 1 - The Docks
- You wake on the pier. Pick up the rusty key from the crate by the net rack.
- Talk to Marta at the fish stall. She gives the quest "Lost Ledger".
- The warehouse door needs the rusty key. Inside there are three crates of coal. You need 3 coal for the furnace in chapter 2, so grab them now.
- Boss: Dockmaster Hale. He charges in a straight line, so dodge sideways and hit him right after the charge. Fire arrows work well in phase 2. Reward: the harbour pass.
- MISSABLE: hand the ledger in to Marta before you leave the docks, she is not there later.

## Chapter 2 - The Furnace Road
- Show the harbour pass to the guard at the gate.
- The furnace needs 3 coal. Light it, then take the iron lift down.
- Tip: the shortcut behind the red barrels skips the whole bridge section.
- Boss: Cinder Warden. Stay behind the pillars when the floor glows. Hit its back plate. It is weak to water. Reward: the lantern key.

## Chapter 3 - The Lantern Tower
- Climb the tower with the lantern key. There are 4 floors, one enemy type per floor: crabs, gulls, rats, then the keeper.
- Keeper fight: the lamp recharges for about 6 seconds after each sweep. That is your window.
- Ending choice: lighting the lamp saves the harbour, breaking it frees the Warden. No point of no return before this choice.
